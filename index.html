How to use it in your car
  1. Park, open this page in the car browser and bookmark it.
  2. Press Start engine and allow location access — the sound follows your GPS speed, so there is no engine without it.
  3. Turn the media volume up; keep your music playing, the engine mixes in on top.
  4. Stopped at a light? Rev blips the throttle. On a phone or laptop, use Test drive and the on-screen pedals.

Expect about half a second of delay inside the car: part of it is the car's audio path, part is GPS only noticing the speed after the fact. A web page cannot drive the outside Boombox speaker — this plays through the cabin speakers like music does.
